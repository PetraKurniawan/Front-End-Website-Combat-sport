Charles "Do Bronx" Oliveira
MMA | Lightweight | Favella

Charles Oliveira, known as "Do Bronx", is a Brazilian MMA fighter and former UFC Lightweight Champion. He holds the record for the most submissions in UFC history. Oliveira combines elite Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu with sharp, evolving striking. Known for his aggressive style, resilience, and ability to finish fights from any position, he remains one of the most dangerous fighters in the division.
